Horizon privacy policy

Last modified: 29 April 2025

This privacy policy outlines how personal information is collected and used by Horizon. By using Horizon Browser you agree to this privacy policy.

Horizon itself does not collect any data. However, since it relies on third-party technology such as WinUI and WebView2, data might be collected by those services. We suggest reading their privacy policies. A full list of all third-party services used is available in the attributions section of the about page.

This privacy policy may be modified or updated at any time without notifying the user. Users should review it periodically.
